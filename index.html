Base vs Brainrots

Lead Programmer
Cactus Studios · Oct 2025 – Dec 2025

Tower defense game published by Cactus! Studios: waves of brainrots attack and players build defenses to hold them off. I built the main systems in Luau:

  • RNG enemy generation: each brainrot gets a random type, rarity and weight; weight scales its size and health.
  • Build mode: building on a 4x4 grid.
  • Defenses: turrets with different attack types and custom effects.
  • Passive income plots: brainrots placed on plots keep earning money even while the player is offline.
  • Cross-server global events: events that run on every server at once, such as lightning storms that grant mutations, plus night and galaxy events.
  • Global shop: shop shared across servers, with limited stock that restocks on a timer.
  • Admin tools: “admin abuse” events to spawn brainrots on every plot or for specific players, with chosen mutations and sizes, give out items and broadcast messages to all servers.
  • Global leaderboards and performance optimization.
